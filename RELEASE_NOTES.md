# What's New in Chimanew v2.6.44

### ☁️ Cross-Device Google Drive Sync (Home Settings & Custom Covers)
* **Full Settings Sync**: Fixed a critical bug in the sync engine where preferences existing on both devices were silently discarded. Now, Chimanew Home preferences (Hero styles, banner URLs, shelf ordering, display options, community ratings) seamlessly merge and sync across all your devices (e.g. phone and laptop) via Google Drive.
* **Custom Artwork & Cover Sync**: When you pick or change a custom cover on your phone, syncing to Google Drive now immediately updates the remote entry version and metadata. Your laptop will automatically detect the updated cover, clear stale cached images, and display the new artwork immediately without manual resets.
* **Instant Home Feed Refresh**: Synchronizing new preferences or covers now immediately triggers an active UI refresh across the Home screen.

### 📱 Tablet & Desktop Navigation Insets
* **Full Left Rail Inset Clearance**: Carried over and reinforced tablet/desktop mode window insets so the vertical Navigation Rail never covers manga shelf headers ("Continue Reading", "Recently Updated"), cards, or hero posters.

### 🔙 Chimanew Home Settings Navigation
* **Dedicated Back Arrow**: One-tap return button in Chimanew Settings returned directly to the Home screen.
