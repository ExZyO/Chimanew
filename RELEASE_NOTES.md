# What's New in Chimanew v2.6.53

### 🔓 Unrestricted Cover Access Across All Ratings
* **Zero Content Restrictions on MangaDex**: Added `contentRating[]=pornographic` to MangaDex cover queries, removing the rating exclusion lock so 18+, adult, and explicit titles can freely resolve and display their volume covers.
* **Doujinshi & Independent Work Support**: Tuned candidate ranking so fan works, self-published series, and doujinshis are never disqualified from cover matching when searching for them or when they are the primary available release.
* **Full Multi-Provider Verification**: Confirmed that all other cover and banner sources (AniList, Kitsu, Bangumi, BookWalker, Naver, and Kuaikan) operate without content rating or adult exclusion filters.
